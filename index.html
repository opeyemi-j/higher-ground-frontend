Our story

Built on trust. Raised on care.

Higher Ground began with a simple idea: young children learn best when they feel completely safe. Every classroom, every routine, and every member of staff is built around that one idea — so curiosity can do the rest.

From nursery play to Primary 6 exams, we walk each child up the same staircase, at a pace that suits them, with a teacher who knows their name and a compound they're excited to run into every morning.

  • Nationally accredited curriculum, taught by qualified teachers
  • CCTV-monitored compound with controlled pickup and drop-off
  • Small class sizes so no child is a face in the crowd

Our promise to parents

You will always know how your child's day went — not guess it. Daily pickup notes for nursery parents, termly reports for primary parents, and a staff room that answers the phone.

The staircase

Three stages. One steady climb.

Each stage builds directly on the one before it, so children never feel a jolt — only a next step.

Step 1

Nursery

Ages 1 – 3

Gentle routines, sensory play, and lots of singing. Our nursery caregivers focus on comfort, language, and first friendships.

Step 2

Kindergarten

Ages 3 – 5

Letters, numbers, and independence through guided play. Children leave kindergarten ready to sit, listen, and ask questions.

Step 3

Primary 1–6

Ages 6 – 11

A full national curriculum in English, Maths, Basic Science, and more — plus clubs in coding, art, and sport every week.
